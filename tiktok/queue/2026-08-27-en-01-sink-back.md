## Caption

If your lower back starts complaining halfway through the dishes, the issue may be how still you are, not how heavy the plates are. A low counter and a small forward lean can leave the same back muscles working the whole time. Change your stance, soften your knees, and give each side a turn. Keep this for the next kitchen reset.
