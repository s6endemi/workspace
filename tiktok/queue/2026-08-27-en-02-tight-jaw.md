## Caption

A tight jaw at the end of a laptop day is easy to miss because the clenching can be tiny. It often shows up when you are concentrating, rushing through emails, or staring at one task too long. Let your teeth separate, lower your shoulders, and take a slower exhale before the tension piles up. Save this for the next screen-heavy afternoon.
