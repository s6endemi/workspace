## Script

If sitting makes one leg tingle, stretch less aggressively first

If a long drive leaves a buzzing strip down one leg, the first instinct is usually to pull hard on the hamstring. That can feel logical. It can also make an already irritated system complain louder.

The sensation may not begin in the spot that feels strange. The sciatic nerve runs from the lower back through the back of the leg. It also has to slide as you sit, bend, and stand. Hours in one bent position can make that whole route less tolerant of movement. This does not tell you the cause of tingling. It does explain why one leg can feel tight, restless, or oddly electric after a train ride while the other feels normal.

Try two gentle resets before a deep stretch. First, stand up and walk slowly for 60 seconds. Let your arms swing and take shorter steps than usual. Then sit tall near the front of a chair. Straighten one knee only until you feel a light pull, point the toes away, then bend the knee again. Do six calm repetitions per side. It should feel like motion, not a test of toughness.

The point is to give the leg a little movement without yanking on it. Many people notice that the urge to stretch hard fades once their back, hip, and leg have changed position a few times.

If tingling is new, severe, follows an injury, comes with weakness, or does not settle, get it checked by a clinician. PREVIA Health is a five-minute movement wellness check that can make asymmetries and compensation patterns easier to spot on your phone.

## Caption

Wenn nach einer langen Fahrt ein Bein kribbelt oder sich elektrisch anfühlt, ist hartes Dehnen nicht immer der beste erste Schritt. Erst kurz gehen, dann das Bein nur sanft bewegen und wieder beugen. So bekommt der ganze Bereich zwischen Rücken, Hüfte und Bein wieder etwas Spielraum. Neu auftretendes Kribbeln, Schwäche oder starke Beschwerden bitte ärztlich abklären lassen. Heb dir das für die nächste lange Fahrt auf.
