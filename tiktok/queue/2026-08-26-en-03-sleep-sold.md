# You were sold a sleep problem before anyone checked your day

**Format:** NotebookLM explanatory video — English audio, 60–90 seconds requested
**Experiment:** System-vs-you / victim-of-bad-information framing. One variable: hook framing. No accusation of fraud or medical claim.

## English source script

You were sold a sleep problem before anyone checked your day.

When you wake up exhausted, the first thing people sell you is a better night: a colder room, a ring that scores you, a supplement, a new mattress, a lamp that turns red at sunset. Some of those things may make your routine nicer. But they can also distract from the most obvious question: did your body get enough movement while it was awake?

Think about a normal workday. Chair. Screen. Car. Couch. Your legs barely load. Your hips barely extend. Your ribcage hardly has a reason to move deeply. Then at night, you expect a body that has spent ten hours in low gear to switch off perfectly on command.

That is not a personal failure. It is what a modern day quietly trains you to do: save movement for the gym, treat fatigue like a settings problem, and buy another tool when the settings do not work.

Before you optimize your bedroom, try changing the day that leads into it. Take a ten-minute walk after lunch. Stand up for two minutes every hour. Do five slow bodyweight squats while your coffee brews. Not as a workout. As a reminder to your nervous system that daytime actually happened.

If you still sleep badly, it is worth getting proper help. But do not let anyone convince you that every tired morning begins in your bedroom.

PREVIA Health shows you where your body has started to compensate after a day of sitting. Five minutes, free, on your phone.
