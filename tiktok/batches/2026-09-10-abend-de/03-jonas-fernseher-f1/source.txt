Why Jonas Walks Away From His Sofa Slightly Crooked

This is Jonas. Jonas has a problem: after two hours of television he stands up and the first steps across his living room come out slightly crooked, every single evening.

Here is what is happening: his sofa faces the window and the television sits over in the corner, so he spends two hours a night sitting turned toward that corner, holding the turn without a single break. He settles into it in the first minute and never notices. Picture Jonas at a long dinner table, seated at the end, turned toward the person on his right for the whole evening: by dessert the turn has stopped feeling like a turn at all, and when he finally stands up he walks off still angled that way for a few steps.

That is why the same happens at his desk, where the second screen sits off to one side all day. It happens again in the car, when he turns to the back seat at every red light. And here is the part almost nobody notices: he always pulls his legs up onto the same side, so it is the same turn every night, for years.

So if this sounds familiar, nothing about you is crooked, you have simply been sitting turned for two hours a night without ever deciding to.
