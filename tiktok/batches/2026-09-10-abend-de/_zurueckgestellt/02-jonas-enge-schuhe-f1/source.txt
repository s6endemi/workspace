Why Jonas's Feet Feel Wrecked After A Short Walk

This is Jonas. Jonas has a problem: after barely fifteen minutes of walking around the city, his feet feel flat and tired, long before his legs or his lungs ever give out.

Here is what is happening: his sneakers squeeze his toes into one narrow point, so they never get to spread out and grip the ground the way they are actually built to. Picture Jonas forced into a pair of rigid ski boots, trying to walk down a hallway with his feet locked stiff and straight, shuffling along in short little steps because nothing below his ankle is allowed to bend or spread out at all.

That is why his feet ache first on every walk, before anything else does. It happens again standing in line at the bakery, where he keeps shifting his weight because his feet cannot find anything to grip onto. And here is the part almost nobody notices: on the one weekend he walked the beach barefoot, his feet felt fine for hours, even on the soft, uneven sand.

So if this sounds familiar, your feet are not weak, they have simply never been allowed to do their actual job.
