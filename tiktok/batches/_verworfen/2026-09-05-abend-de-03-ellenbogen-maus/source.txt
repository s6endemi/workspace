Why your elbow burns and you never work out

You do not play any sport, and your elbow still burns on the outside. You were never told why. Stay with me, this is quick.

All day at your desk your wrist rests tilted slightly upward over the mouse, barely enough to notice. The muscles on top of your forearm hold that tiny lift for eight hours straight, pulling from the exact spot on the outside of your elbow. It is like a handbrake left on while the car is parked, quietly holding tension that never gets to release, hour after hour.

Previa Health shows you in a short test whether your wrist is really the one staying tilted all day.

You know the pattern. Picking up a coffee cup and feeling it right on the outside of your elbow. Shaking hands a little too firmly and wincing. Turning a doorknob feels sharper than it should. And especially the third fix below, almost nobody does it, and it is the one that finally lets the forearm let go of the tension it held all day.

First, move your mouse closer so your whole forearm rests on the desk and your wrist stays flat, and check this three times a day. Second, straighten your arm and stretch your wrist down and toward you for twenty seconds on each side. If you want more small tricks like this, feel free to follow Previa Health. Third, in the evening, open and close your fist ten times with your fingers spread wide. Nothing in your elbow is broken. It just held a tiny lift for eight hours straight, and it finally said something.
