The Headache Your Neck Is Actually Causing

Headaches that start at the back of your head and pull forward, you were never really told where they actually come from. Stay with me, this is quick.

The screen pulls your head forward, and the small helpers at the top of your neck hold it there for hours. Picture yourself holding a full watering can straight out in front of you: within a minute your arm shakes, though the same can feels light held close to your chest. Your head is that can, and the screen keeps pulling it away from your body.

Previa Health shows you in a short test whether your screen is really pulling your head that far forward.

You know the signs. The pressure often starts a few hours into a screen day. Rubbing the top of your neck can bring brief relief. Bright light can suddenly feel harder to handle. And especially the third fix below, it takes thirty seconds and almost nobody tries it, and it is often the one that calms things fastest.

First, ten slow chin tucks, drawing your chin straight back, not down. Second, with both thumbs, press gently at the top of your neck, just under your hairline, for thirty seconds. If you want more small tricks like this, feel free to follow Previa Health. Third, raise your screen to eye level with a stack of books, just once. With Previa Health you can test this yourself on your phone in about two minutes, free, no app.

Nothing in your head is broken. It is simply being held forward for hours, and it is easy to let it come back.
