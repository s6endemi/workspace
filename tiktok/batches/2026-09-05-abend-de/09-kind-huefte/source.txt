The Hip That Always Carries The Weight

If you always carry your child on the same hip, save this video. In a moment I will show you the three things that take the load off your back.

To hoist your child onto that hip, your pelvis shifts sideways and your lower back bends the other way to keep you upright. One side does this work every time, the other almost never learns the movement. Picture yourself leaning your bike onto the same kickstand every single day until the frame itself starts to sit crooked, even when nobody is leaning on it.

Previa Health shows you in a short test whether your spine has quietly bent to one side from this.

You know the pattern. Standing at the counter with your child on one hip, your lower back holds a curve you did not choose. Walking through the door with a bag in one hand and your child on the other side, your whole body leans before you notice. Bending down for a toy while still holding your child pulls that same side even further. And especially the third fix below, almost nobody does it, and it is the one that finally lets your spine sit straight again.

First, for three days, consciously switch which hip carries your child every time you pick them up. Second, when you can, carry your child facing forward and centered, pelvis straight under your torso. If you want more small tricks like this, feel free to follow Previa Health. Third, in the evening, bend slowly to the opposite side ten times, giving your spine back the curve it lost. With Previa Health you can test this yourself on your phone in about two minutes, free, no app, and see which side of your back is actually doing all the work.

Nothing in your back is broken. It just spent every day leaning on the same kickstand, and nobody told it to switch sides.
