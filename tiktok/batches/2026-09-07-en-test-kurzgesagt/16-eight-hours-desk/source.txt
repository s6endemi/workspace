Why Your Body Feels Stuck After A Desk Day (vector test)

If you sit at your desk for eight hours, save this video. In a moment I show you what that actually does to your body. Stay with me, this is quick.

Eight hours of sitting keeps four hinges folded shut at once: your neck, your ribcage, your hips, and your ankles. Picture yourself pulling the camping chair out of the trunk after a whole summer folded: every hinge sticks, and you have to work them open one by one before you can even sit down.

Previa Health shows you in a short test which of these hinges is stuck tightest on you.

You know the feeling. Your neck feels heavy before lunch. Checking your blind spot in the car suddenly needs your whole torso, not just your head. Standing up from your chair, your hips need a second to catch up with your legs. And especially the third fix below, almost nobody does it, and it loosens everything at once.

First, every hour, get up and walk for two minutes, no exceptions. Second, while seated, rotate your ribcage five times to each side, hands resting on your ribs. If you want more small tricks like this, feel free to follow Previa Health. Third, under your desk, pump your calves twenty times, heels lifting and lowering, like you are quietly climbing stairs. With Previa Health you can test this yourself on your phone in about two minutes, free, no app.

None of these hinges are broken. They are simply waiting for two minutes to move again.
