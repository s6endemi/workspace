Why Jonas's Leg Feels Heavy After Every Commute

This is Jonas. Jonas has a problem: every time he parks after his commute, his right leg feels stiff and heavy, and the first few steps come out slow and clumsy.

Here is what is happening: his right foot never really rests on the pedal, it stays half lifted the entire drive, ready to brake, and his leg never lets go of that half ready position. Picture Jonas standing on a crowded bus that keeps stopping and starting, balancing with his legs alone and never grabbing the rail: after twenty minutes his legs have done real work without a single step.

That is why his leg feels the same after a long red light, foot pressed on the brake without moving an inch. It happens again in the parking garage, circling for a spot with his foot hovering between the pedals. And here is the part almost nobody notices: on the days his partner drives instead, Jonas gets out and his legs feel completely normal, right from the first step.

So if this sounds familiar, your leg is not weak, it has simply been working the entire drive without you ever noticing it.
