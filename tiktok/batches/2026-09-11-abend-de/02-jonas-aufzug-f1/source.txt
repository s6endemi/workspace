Why Jonas Only Finds Out On The Day The Lift Is Broken

This is Jonas. Jonas has a problem: he has taken the lift in the car park for years, and on the one morning it is out of order, two floors of stairs leave him standing on the landing, hand on the rail, wondering what just happened.

Here is what is happening: stairs are the one thing in his day that asks one leg to lift his whole weight, and he has not asked for that in a very long time. Picture Jonas at the station on a morning when the escalator is taped off: he starts up the long staircase at his usual brisk pace, and by the halfway landing he is taking the steps one at a time and letting two people past him.

That is why the second flight up to his flat is the slow one after a big shop. It happens on the beach steps on holiday, where he stops at the top and blames the heat. And here is the part almost nobody notices: he did not lose this on any particular day, he lost it two floors at a time, over years, without one moment that felt like a loss.

So if this sounds familiar, nothing about your legs is giving out, they simply have not been asked to lift you in a long time.
