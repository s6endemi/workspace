Why Jonas Never Takes The Half Step In His Own Kitchen

This is Jonas. Jonas has a problem: he cooks every evening, and afterwards he is stiffer than after the walk home, even though his kitchen is four steps wide.

Here is what is happening: he never takes those four steps. He parks himself in front of the hob and reaches for everything, leaning across the worktop for the salt, hanging into the fridge for the milk at the back, stretching sideways to the drawer with one heel off the floor. Picture Jonas lying on a hotel bed, reaching for a charger on the desk: he stretches off the mattress edge, fingertips just short of it, holding that stretch far longer than it deserves, until he gets up, takes two steps and has it in his hand.

That is why the jar he actually wanted is the one he gives up on. It happens at his desk too, where the printer is one step away and he leans. And here is the part almost nobody notices: on the evenings a friend is over and he walks around the kitchen talking, he never thinks about his back at all.

So if this sounds familiar, nothing about you is stiff, you simply stopped taking the half step and let the leaning do it.
