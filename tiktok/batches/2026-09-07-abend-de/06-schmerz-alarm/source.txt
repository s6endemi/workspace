Your Pain Is An Alarm, Not A Damage Report

Pain does not show you where something is broken. It shows you where your body is sounding an alarm.

Pain is a warning your body builds out of how tired you are, how much you have done, and what you expect to happen next, so a stiff, overworked spot can scream loudly with nothing damaged, while a truly hurt spot can stay completely silent. Picture yourself in a kitchen where the smoke alarm goes off because the toast is a little dark: nothing is burning, the alarm is just set very sensitive.

That is why your back can hurt more after a rough, stressful day than after a day you actually lifted something heavy. It is also why being afraid to move can turn the alarm up even louder without you noticing. Even the mood you are in can turn the sensitivity up or down before you take a single step. And here is the part worth waiting for: movement that feels safe is one of the few things that can turn that same alarm back down again.
