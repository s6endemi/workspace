The Free Energy Hiding In Every Step You Take

Half of every step you take is supposed to be free, and most people stopped cashing it in.

With every step, the tendon at the back of your ankle stretches under your weight and stores energy like a spring, then hands that energy straight back to push you forward, almost for free. Picture yourself bouncing on a trampoline: the mat does half the work for you, until someone lays a heavy mattress on top of it, and suddenly every single jump costs you everything.

That's why shuffling instead of walking briskly tires you out so much faster, since you stop letting that spring do any work at all and your muscles have to cover the difference. It's also why people quietly lose that bounce as they get older and start moving in that careful, heavy way everyone notices. And here's the one runners obsess over: that same spring is exactly what gives a fast walker or a runner their bounce back with every single stride.

So here's what actually helps. Take twenty brisk steps where you consciously push off through your big toe instead of just shuffling forward flat footed.
