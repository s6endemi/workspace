Your Muscles Never Get Longer When You Stretch

Stretching does not make your muscles longer. It talks your brain into something, and that changes everything.

How far you can stretch has almost nothing to do with the length of your muscle. It is decided by a brake your brain pulls the moment a position feels unsafe, and calm, repeated stretching loosens that brake, not the tissue. Picture yourself pushing a heavy garden gate that is not stuck at all: a nervous dog on the other side keeps leaning against it, and the moment the dog trusts you, the gate swings open.

That is why you can suddenly reach further after warming up, even though your muscle has not changed one bit. It is also why stress can make you feel stiffer within minutes, before you have even moved. You can even feel it shift from morning to evening, your body allows more the calmer it feels, not the looser your tissue somehow became overnight. And the strangest part comes right after: kids touch their toes so easily not because their muscles are special, but because their brake barely holds anything back.
