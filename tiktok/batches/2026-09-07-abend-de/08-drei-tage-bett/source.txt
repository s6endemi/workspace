Bed Rest Costs You More Muscle Than Training Builds

Three days in bed can cost you more muscle than three weeks of training can ever bring back.

Muscle that has nothing to carry gets marked by your body as extra weight it no longer wants to pay for, and it disappears fast, while building it back is always slow. Picture yourself running a small bakery: the day you stop selling bread, you send the bakers home that same evening, but hiring and training new ones takes weeks before the ovens are back to full speed. Your body treats muscle exactly the same way, because keeping it fed and ready costs energy every single day, and your body is always hunting for a discount.

That is why a few days of flu can leave your legs wobbling on the stairs the moment you finally get up. It is why the water crate feels heavier the week after a lazy vacation than it did before you left. And here is the part that catches people off guard, so stay with this: the older you get, the faster that bakery closes and the slower it reopens, so the gap between a pause and a comeback keeps growing wider every year.
