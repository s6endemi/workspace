Why Your Whole Body Feels Stuck At Dawn

Every morning, everything inside your body is stuck together just a little, and that is why you wake up stiff. The thin layers wrapped around every muscle need movement to stay slippery. Overnight, and after long stretches of sitting still, those layers settle against each other, and your very first movements are what peels them apart again.

Picture yourself peeling two sheets of cling film apart that have been lying on top of each other on the counter overnight: they stick at first, resist for a second, then slide freely once they are finally moving. Your body runs the exact same trick every single morning, just with living layers instead of plastic.

The first ten steps out of bed are always the stiffest steps of your entire day, before anything has had the chance to slide. Sit through a long film at the cinema and you stand up afterward like you aged decades in two hours. And here is the part that surprises most people: a heating pad alone will never undo it, only actual movement will.
