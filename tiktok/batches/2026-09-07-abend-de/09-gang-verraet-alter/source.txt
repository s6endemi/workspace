The Way You Walk Reveals Your Real Age

The way you walk gives away how old your body really is, and it only takes three seconds to see it. How fast do you walk compared to your friends? Tell me in the comments.

Walking at a brisk pace needs real strength in your push-off, steady balance, and a hip that swings open behind you with every step. The moment even one of those three fades a little, your step gets shorter and slower long before anyone would ever call it age. Picture yourself walking next to a friend down the platform as the train doors start to close: one of you lengthens the stride and reaches back with each step to make it through, while the other breaks into three quick shuffles and simply stands there watching the doors shut.

A shorter step shows up long before a slower step does, which is why most people miss it in themselves for years. Walking up a moving escalator instead of just standing on it quietly disappears from your routine first. And here is the part almost nobody notices about themselves, so keep watching: your arms stop swinging with your stride long before your legs actually slow down.
