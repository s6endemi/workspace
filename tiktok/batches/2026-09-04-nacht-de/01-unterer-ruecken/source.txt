Your lower back hurts and nobody can tell you why

You have lower back pain and you do not know where it comes from? Then listen very closely, because today I explain it to you. You did not know this? Then this is exactly for you.

Your lower back is rarely the culprit. It is the loudest. Picture three people carrying a sofa. The one in the middle is your lower back. The two at the ends are your hips and your ribcage. When those two stop moving, after a day in the car, at the desk, on the sofa, the one in the middle carries everything alone. He does not fail. He complains.

Previa Health shows you in a short test which of the two has stopped helping.

And you know the signs. The back feels stiff when you get up from a chair. Turning around in the car pulls on one side. Standing at the sink for five minutes feels worse than walking for twenty. And especially number three of the fixes, almost nobody does it, and it is the one that changes the most.

First, seated rotations. Sit tall, cross your arms, and rotate slowly five times each way. Feel whether your ribcage turns before your hips follow. Second, the half-kneeling hip glide. One knee down, stay upright, and push the hips gently forward for thirty seconds each side. Follow Previa Health so you do not miss the next body signal. Third, the wall ankle test. Face a wall, keep your heel down and bring the knee toward the wall ten times per side. If one side stops early, that is your carrier who let go.

With Previa Health you can test this yourself on your phone in about two minutes, free, no app, and see which neighbour stopped carrying.

Nothing in your back is broken. It is doing two jobs. Give the others theirs back.
