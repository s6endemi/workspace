Your soft sofa is making your evenings stiffer, not easier

Your sofa does not relax you in the evening. It leaves you stiffer than your whole workday did, and the furniture industry will never tell you why. You did not know that? Then listen very closely.

The industry keeps selling softer cushions as if sinking deeper were the fix. Picture your sofa as a swamp. The deep cushion pulls your pelvis backward, so your hips tuck under and your ribcage caves forward, and both lock there for two hours straight. Nothing rotates, nothing carries weight. That is not rest. Joints that sit still that long feel years older the moment you get up.

Previa Health shows you in a short test whether your evenings are actually resting your body or quietly locking it.

You know the signs. Getting up from the sofa takes a small run up. Your lower back cracks on the first steps to the kitchen. Socks feel tighter to put on than they did that morning. And especially the third fix, almost nobody does it, and it undoes more than the whole evening on the cushion.

First, every twenty minutes, stand up and simply stand for thirty seconds. Second, slide your sitting bones forward to the front edge of the sofa for one minute, spine tall, feet flat. Follow Previa Health so you do not miss the next body signal. Third, before bed, do ten slow sit to stands from the sofa without using your hands.

With Previa Health you can test this yourself on your phone in about two minutes, free, no app.

You paid maybe two thousand euros for a softer sofa. It cannot do what two minutes of standing does for free. Nothing about your evening body is broken. It just spent two hours pretending to rest.
