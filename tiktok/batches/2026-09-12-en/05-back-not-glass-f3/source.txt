Your Back Was Never As Fragile As You Think

Your back is not made of glass. It is just being treated like it is one.

A back that is never allowed to carry, bend or twist does not become safer, it simply forgets how, and then it reacts to the smallest things instead of the big ones. Loading it a little, again and again, is what keeps it capable, not protecting it from everything. Picture yourself picking a toddler up off the floor twenty times a day without even thinking about it, and then one day carrying a bag of groceries in from the car like it is full of raw eggs, because someone once told you to be careful.

That is why people who guard their back constantly still feel it the moment they bend down to tie a shoe. It is why people who keep moving normally, lifting, bending, twisting a little every day, notice it far less often. And the strangest part is that being afraid of a movement can make you stiffer than the movement itself ever would.
