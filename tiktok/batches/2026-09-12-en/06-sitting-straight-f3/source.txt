Sitting Perfectly Straight Is The Hardest Way To Sit

Sitting perfectly straight is not the healthiest way to sit. It is actually the most exhausting one your back can choose.

A back locked into one straight position the entire time does not get protected, it gets tired, because the same small muscles have to hold that exact shape without any help, minute after minute. Changing position again and again is what actually spreads the work around, not staying rigid the whole time. Picture yourself standing guard outside a building, told to stay perfectly straight and still for a full hour: by the end you are swaying on your feet and aching everywhere, while the guard next to you, quietly shifting weight from one foot to the other the whole time, still looks completely fine.

That is why sitting up ramrod straight at a desk can leave you sorer by lunch than slouching ever did. It is why you can copy the perfect posture off a poster and lose it again before the end of your first email. And here is the part that surprises people: changing position badly, again and again, beats holding one perfect position still.
