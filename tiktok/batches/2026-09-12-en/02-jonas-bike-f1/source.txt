Why Jonas Walks Into The Office Still Bent Forward

This is Jonas. Jonas has a problem: every time he gets off his bike after riding to work, he walks into the building like he never actually got off the saddle.

Here is what is happening: an hour folded forward over the handlebars teaches his body a shape, and his body quietly keeps that shape after he stands up. Picture Jonas carrying a heavy box down the office hallway, leaning back the whole way to balance it, then setting it down in the corner and walking on for a few more steps still leaning back, as if the box were still in his arms.

That is why his very first step off the bike is short and choppy, not a full stride. Minutes later, standing at the coffee machine in the kitchen, his hips keep sliding forward instead of stacking under him. And here is the part almost nobody notices: by the end of the day, it is his lower back quietly burning, even though his legs did all the pedaling.

So if this sounds familiar, your body is not stiff, it is simply still holding the bike position long after you parked it.
