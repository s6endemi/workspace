Why Long Flights Make You Walk Off Like You're Eighty

If you're flying tomorrow, or stuck on a long bus ride, stay with me for a second. I'm about to give you three things you can do so you don't step off feeling eighty.

Here's the mechanic: your legs only move blood back upward when you actually move them, and folded into a seat for hours, nothing moves at all. Picture yourself in the parking lot at the airport, wrestling a folded camping chair out of a packed trunk, the hinges stuck shut after a whole winter in the garage, pushing it open with both hands before it finally gives. That is what standing up in the aisle feels like four hours later, while everyone behind you waits.

First thing: every forty five minutes, lift your heels off the floor and set them back down twenty times, quiet enough that nobody notices. And the third one, almost nobody does this: the second you land, before your suitcase even shows up on the belt, drop into a half kneel for thirty seconds right there, or against a wall if that feels less strange. That one resets your hips faster than anything else on this list.
