Jonas Bought A Standing Desk And Nothing Changed

This is Jonas. Jonas has a problem: he bought a standing desk to fix his back, and after eight hours on his feet, his back feels exactly the same as before.

Here is what is happening: standing still for hours is just as much of a freeze as sitting still for hours, his body does not need a new place to hold still, it needs to move between positions. Picture Jonas standing at his desk like a museum guard who is not allowed to move, his coffee going cold next to the keyboard, one hand on the desk after twenty minutes and his weight parked on one hip after forty.

That is why after two hours he is hanging his whole weight into one hip without even noticing it. Soon after, his heels start to burn against the hard floor. And here is the part almost nobody notices: by the evening, he is more tired from standing all day than he ever was from sitting.

So if this sounds familiar, the desk was never the real problem, staying frozen in one position was.

Every thirty minutes, switch between standing and sitting, even on the days you do not feel like it yet. If you want more of this, feel free to follow Previa Health.

And while you are standing, shift your weight from foot to foot every few minutes, or rest one foot on a small step or a thick book.
