You have been breathing wrong for years and you never noticed it. Stay with me, this is quick.

Most adults only breathe into the top of the chest. The collarbones lift, the shoulders creep up, and the ribs barely move sideways at all, even though your ribcage is built like a bucket handle that should swing outward with every inhale. Years of sitting hunched over a screen teach your body to skip that motion entirely, and the deep breathing muscles around your ribs slowly forget their job.

Previa Health shows you in a short test how much your ribcage actually moves when you breathe.

You know the signs. You sigh a lot without noticing. A deep breath feels like it gets stuck halfway. Your shoulders and neck feel tight by the end of the day, even on days you barely moved them. And especially the third fix, almost nobody does this one, and it is the one that resets the whole pattern the fastest.

First, place your hands flat on the sides of your ribs and breathe directly into your hands for five breaths, feeling them push outward. Second, exhale slowly through pursed lips with a light hissing sound, five times, letting the ribs drop fully each time. If you want more small tricks like this, feel free to follow Previa Health. Third, lie face down, rest your forehead on your hands, and breathe into your lower back and sides for one minute, feeling the ribcage expand into the floor.

With Previa Health you can test this yourself on your phone in about two minutes, free, no app, and see how far your ribcage actually opens.

Nothing in your chest is broken. It just forgot a movement it used to make on its own, every single day.
