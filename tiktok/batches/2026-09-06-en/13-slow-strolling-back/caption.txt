A slow stroll can wreck your back worse than a whole hike. Real walking lets the hip swing all the way through, but strolling stops it halfway, so your lower back locks up and carries your torso alone. Brisk steps between stops, shifting your weight at shop windows, and a half kneeling lunge at night give the hip its stride back.
