Dinner prep can tire your shoulders without a single heavy lift. Chopping, stirring, reaching, and looking down all keep your arms and upper back doing quiet work for longer than you notice. A few small movement breaks can change how that evening feels.

Keep this one in mind the next time you are rubbing your shoulders before the food is even on the table.
