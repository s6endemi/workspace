## Text (269/280)

An easy dashboard is a low bar.

Across 164 sensor-based health tools, 91% checked if people found the app easy to use. Only 9% checked if people understood their data or knew what to do next.

A score that doesn’t help you do anything tomorrow is just a pretty screen.
